About us

Caring for dogs and cats since 1980

Hudora Pet Hotel opened in 1980. We sit in open countryside at Adlington, near Chorley, just a mile from the M61, so we're a short drive from Wigan, Bolton, Chorley, Horwich, Bury and Manchester.

We know how hard it is to leave your pet behind. That's why each dog and cat gets our time and attention, so they feel as much at home as possible. Many vets and their staff trust us with their own pets, and we'd love to show you round.
