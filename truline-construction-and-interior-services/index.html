Recent projects

Spaces we've finished

A few recent jobs from our portfolio, and what the clients said about them.

Education · Summer 2023

Hugh Baird College atrium

The college needed its Atrium, Mezzanine and library areas upgraded before the new term began. Our design team worked with college staff on a brighter space with biophilic touches, including a moss wall and a large fish tank, then we mobilised quickly to get it done in time.

"The communication was strong from start to finish with great management on site ensuring a high quality of workmanship throughout to deliver an excellent service."Hugh Baird College, client feedback

Education · May 2023

Student Life Building, LJMU

We fitted out the Counselling and Mental Health Hub at Liverpool John Moores University, adding glazed partitions, acoustics and access control for more privacy. The building stayed open, so we phased the work to keep disruption to staff and students down.

"The end results of the refurbishment works in the Student Life Building are fantastic and the university will get lots of use from the new more private areas."Client feedback, Liverpool John Moores University

Education and hospitality

Unit One, MMU

We turned an existing space at 'The Union' at MMU into a café and bar for students and locals. It was a complete design and fit out built around the brand's signature yellow, from M&E and lighting to bespoke furniture, flooring and WCs.

"No issue has been too much to deal with and the flexibility and early starts associated with furniture deliveries have been pivotal to the success of the project."Andy Potter, Manchester Metropolitan University

About us

A Wigan team working across the North West

Truline is a commercial design and build contractor based at Martland Park in Wigan. We have 35 years' experience in construction, refurbishment, fit out, planned maintenance and bespoke joinery, working for offices, schools, universities, healthcare, shops and cafés across Manchester, Liverpool and the North West.

There are over 40 of us, led by our Chairman Ian Lucas and Managing Director Mike Hyde. We have our own trades, an in-house interior designer and a commercial team, and we put real effort into apprenticeships. Every month we recognise someone with the Spirit of Truline award, and six of the team took on Born Survivor, raising £1,610 for Cash for Kids.
