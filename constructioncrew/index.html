What we do

From a weld repair to a full biogas plant

We work on small jobs and large ones. Most of our work is in the biogas sector, but our team also takes on general construction, fabrication and plumbing.

Biogas plant maintenance

Gas membrane inspections and changes, digester cleaning (de-grit) and mechanical troubleshooting and repairs. We keep your plant running safely and efficiently.

Get a quote

General maintenance

Routine inspections, servicing and repairs on pumps, compressors, valves and gas storage systems. We also handle debris removal, oil changes and lubrication.

Get a quote

Fabrication and welding

Steel and aluminium welding, repairs and fabrication of pre-assembled components. We take on contract work and production runs for small to medium-sized parts.

Industrial pipework

Pipe and plant installations carried out by our own pipe fitters, welders and mechanical engineers.

About us

A Manchester firm led by Janis Gertmanis

ConstructionCrew is a Manchester-based construction firm. Our work ranges from basic welding to multi-million pound anaerobic digestion facilities, for clients in the UK and abroad.

The company is led by Janis Gertmanis, CEO and Managing Director, alongside Sanita, Janis, Luke and Lewis. Our team has over a decade of experience, and we care about quality, safety and building lasting relationships with our clients.
